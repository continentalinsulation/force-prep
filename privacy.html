Evolution — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Evolution is a daily training and discipline app. This policy explains what the app stores and why.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and a password (stored hashed by our authentication provider). We never see your password.
  • Profile: the name and username you choose. These are visible to people in your crews and to anyone who adds you by username.
  • Training data: your program, completed sessions, routine check-ins, test simulation times, personal bests, notes, and settings. A summary (streaks, sessions completed, best push-up count, best sim time) is visible to your crew and friends so that leaderboards and challenges work.
  • Activity feed: short entries such as "secured Lift · foundation in 28:40" are shown to your crew and friends.

What we don't collect

  • No location, contacts, photos, health-app data, or advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs. No ads.

Where it's stored

Data is stored on your device and synced to a database hosted by Supabase (supabase.com), protected by row-level security so each account can only read and write its own records plus the shared crew summaries described above.

Who can see it

Only you, and the people in crews you join or who you add as friends, for the summary fields listed above. We do not sell or share data with third parties.

Deleting your account

In the app: Me → Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your account, your training data, your profile, your crew memberships, and your activity entries. You can also email us (below) and we will delete it within 7 days.

Children

Evolution is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
