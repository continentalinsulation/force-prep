## Description (4000)
Evolution is a training log for people who are preparing for something.

Pick what you're here for and the app builds the program around it:

MILITARY
• FORCE Prep — the CAF fitness test, trained specifically: sandbag stations, timed simulations, a countdown to test day.
• BMQ Prep — 8 weeks of push-ups, sit-ups, pull-ups and running volume so basic is about learning, not surviving.
• Selection Prep — 12 weeks for special-forces candidates: pull-up and push-up ladders, rucks that get heavier and longer, intervals, long runs, swims.
• Rucking — 12 weeks from 4 km at 10 kg to 20 km at 20 kg.

RUNNING AND ENGINE
• Couch to 5K, 10K, Half marathon, Marathon — real week-by-week progressions with a taper and a race day.
• Cycling / Peloton — endurance, sweet spot, sprints and a long ride that reaches two hours.
• Build an engine — intervals and longer efforts with two strength days.

STRENGTH AND BODY
• Get strong, Get lean, Strong over 40.
• Postpartum rebuild — breathing, deep core and pelvic floor first, then strength, at your pace. No jumping or heavy loading until the foundation is back, and clear stop rules on every session.

EVERYDAY
• Just build the habit — twenty minutes, daily.
• Desk reset — fifteen minutes of hips, back and neck.

THE LOGBOOK
Every lift is a set grid: what you did last time, a target for today based on your own numbers, kg, reps, tick. Tick a set and the rest timer starts. Beat your best and the row flashes. Swap any exercise for the day with one tap. Timed and interval work runs with a guided countdown, beeps and haptics.

THE ROUTINE
Evolution is built around a fixed wake time and the night before: alarm across the room, clothes laid out, lights out on time. Morning routine chips, a lights-out reminder, and a streak for the routine itself — not just the training.

THE CREW
Start a crew with a six-letter code. See who's done today, run challenges (most sessions, longest streak, push-up max, best FORCE sim), and share a card when you secure an evolution.

YOUR KIT
Tell it what you have — sandbag, dumbbells, kettlebell, barbell, rack, bands, bike, rower, pull-up bar, and twenty more — and every session is built from it. 220 exercises, each with cues, common mistakes and a video.

Finishing is a moment. The app calls each session an evolution and counts them. Secure today's.
