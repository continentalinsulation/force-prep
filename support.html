Getting started

Create an account, pick a program (FORCE Prep, Base, Strength, Run), tap the kit you own, and your first evolution is on the Today tab. Change any day in Plan.

Common questions

My streak didn't count. A day counts when every exercise in the session is ticked, or when you finish it in guided mode and tap Secure. Rest days never break a streak.

I switched phones. Sign in with the same email and your data comes down from the cloud.

No beeps. Check the phone's silent switch, then Me → Settings → Beeps.

Delete my account. Me → Settings → Delete account.
